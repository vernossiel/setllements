The building templates in this folder are converted from MineColonies blueprints
(https://github.com/ldtteam/minecolonies), licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0.
Style "nordic" by Luna and John Reiher (MineColonies schematic builders) (see the "credit" field in each file).
Converted to vanilla blocks, turned to face south and cropped; the timber is swapped for the
village's own wood when built. MineColonies-only blocks were replaced by vanilla equivalents.

The building templates in this folder are converted from MineColonies blueprints
(https://github.com/ldtteam/minecolonies), licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0. The designs are the work of
the MineColonies schematic builders; each file names its style and authors in its "credit" field:
  nordic          - Luna, John Reiher
  medievaloak,
  medievalbirch,
  medievaldarkoak - Carlansor
  sandstone       - Raycoms, John Reiher, SomeAddons, AravanFox, Moobien
Changes made for Settlements: converted to vanilla blocks (Domum Ornamentum's shaped blocks become the
vanilla stairs, slabs, walls, fences and doors of their material, its bricks the nearest vanilla stone),
MineColonies' own blocks replaced by vanilla ones, turned to face south and cropped; when built, the
timber is swapped for the village's own wood.
